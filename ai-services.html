Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers regarding enterprise security, integration timelines, and expected return on investment.

How does Medhavat protect our company's confidential data?

We strictly operate under zero-data-retention agreements. Client data is never used to train public foundational models. We deploy isolated single-tenant vector stores, AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, role-based access control (RBAC), and support 100% on-premise air-gapped private VPC deployments.

What is the typical timeline for an AI implementation?

We deploy a working, benchmarked proof-of-concept (MVP) in 3 to 4 weeks. Deep enterprise integration with existing ERPs, legacy databases, guardrails, and production MLOps workflows completes within 8 to 12 weeks.

Can your AI connect to our existing custom ERP and databases?

Yes. We engineer bi-directional connectors for PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, SAP, Salesforce, Oracle, HubSpot, Jira, and proprietary legacy databases with real-time audit logging and transactional safety rollbacks.

How do you eliminate AI hallucinations and control token costs?

We employ strict deterministic grounding, hybrid vector-lexical search (BM25 + dense embeddings), cross-encoder re-ranking, and dynamic semantic caching. Unverifiable queries automatically trigger confidence fallbacks or route to human-in-the-loop review.

Can we deploy AI models on our private cloud or on-premise infrastructure?

Yes. In addition to hosted APIs (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini), we specialize in fine-tuning and hosting open-weight models (Llama 3, Mistral, Gemma) on your own AWS Bedrock, GCP Vertex AI, or on-premise GPU servers (using vLLM and Ollama) for zero cloud-egress compliance.

Do you offer dedicated AI developers or turnkey delivery?

We offer both. You can commission Medhavat for a complete turnkey product with fixed milestones and guaranteed SLAs, or hire a dedicated AI & Data engineering pod that integrates seamlessly with your in-house product team.
